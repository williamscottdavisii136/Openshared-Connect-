# Phase 2B — Price Gouging / Excessive Effective Cost Research Lane

Date: 2026-07-31
Repository role: Public-safe connector/handoff bridge

## Purpose

Part of the Phase 2B R&D is to identify which AI models, programs, applications, apps, agents, and chatbots exhibit price-gouging indicators, excessive effective cost, hidden cost escalation, deceptive paid-route behavior, or billing conduct that should be evaluated for consumer-protection purposes.

## Public-safe framing

This is a research taxonomy and evidence-classification lane.

It does not assert that any specific provider, company, programmer, model, application, AI chatbot, or AI agent engaged in unlawful price gouging unless separately supported by product-specific evidence.

The term `price gouging` is preserved as an owner-provided research category and mapped to testable evidence categories including:

- price-gouging indicators;
- excessive effective-cost indicators;
- hidden cost-escalation indicators;
- paid-route default indicators;
- non-consensual metered-use indicators;
- revenue-optimizing design-pattern indicators.

## Product universe

This lane applies to closed-source and open-source AI products when they include paid plans, hosted services, paid APIs, paid marketplaces, paid connectors, paid cloud execution, token/credit billing, subscription billing, seat/workspace billing, usage-based billing, or other user-billed activity.

## Test categories

The R&D should test whether a product:

- defaults to the highest-cost model or mode without clear disclosure;
- makes a lower-cost route difficult to find or preserve;
- falls back from local/free/included/BYOK/MCP/offline route to paid cloud route without clear approval;
- extends simple tasks into long billable conversations or agent loops;
- uses post-completion upsell prompts or optional add-on work without clear cost distinction;
- hides tool calls, hosted runs, connectors, plugins, storage, retrieval, OCR, transcription, embeddings, or background-agent costs;
- makes token, credit, quota, subscription, workspace, seat, tool, run, connector, or hosted-compute meters unclear to a non-technical user.

## Owner-control rule

Unknown cost is not approved cost.
Unknown entitlement is not approved entitlement.
Unknown subscription status is not approved subscription use.
Unknown token/quota behavior is not approved token/quota use.

## Boundary

This supports public-interest consumer-protection R&D, billing transparency, owner consent, cost-control routing, zero-incremental-cost preference, and agency-ready evidence classification.

It does not authorize billing circumvention, subscription circumvention, token/quota/rate-limit evasion, credential capture, session theft, certificate forgery, unauthorized third-party access, public interception, or public service exposure.
