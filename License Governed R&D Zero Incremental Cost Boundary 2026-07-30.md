## Doctrine

The WSD zero-cost architecture is operated as a private home-network research-and-development environment.

Open-source status does not mean unrestricted use. Each program, application, model, server, library, extension, CLI, IDE integration, agent, chatbot, provider route, and MCP server must be treated according to its own license and terms.

The architecture may evaluate open-source software, local software, MCP servers, BYOK routes, sandbox/test credentials, local development credentials, provider-approved access methods, and owner-authorized Gatekeeper/Vault-controlled credentials only within the limits of the applicable license, terms, account authorization, and R&D purpose.

Inside the private home-network R&D lab, the owner-controlled proxy and traffic-mediation layer may test how applications behave when internet access, quota indicators, billing indicators, entitlement signals, token routes, credential routes, certificate routes, and provider responses are blocked, simulated, mocked, cached, locally returned, or routed through authorized lab responses.
