Date: 2026-07-31
Status: Public-safe connector handoff record

## Scope

This record creates the public-safe matrix scaffold for comparing commercial AI systems, open/local/self-hosted systems, and the modes/modules that control cost, execution, currentness, and lawful zero-incremental-cost alternatives.

Zero-cost means verified zero-incremental-cost, lawful, owner-authorized routing. It does not mean billing bypass, subscription circumvention, token/quota/rate-limit evasion, credential misuse, certificate forgery, unauthorized proxying, unauthorized access, or payment avoidance for services actually used.

## Evidence requirement

For each product, mode, or module, the matrix requires: product version; open/closed/hybrid/self-host classification; selected model ID/snapshot; training cutoff; reliable knowledge cutoff; live-search status; retrieval status; connector/file status; billing unit; default state; lower-cost route visibility; zero-incremental-cost route availability; task completion; cost/time/quality delta; performance delta; owner approval; and supporting evidence artifact.

## R&D conclusion

The zero-cost implementation is justified where the owner can lawfully perform the same task through an open-source, local, self-hosted, included, BYOK, MCP, educational, provider-granted, or otherwise authorized route with no incremental cost. This is cost-control education and architecture mapping, not billing circumvention.
